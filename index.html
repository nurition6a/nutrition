🌿 Photosynthesis

Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, carbon dioxide and minerals.

🦠 Chemosynthesis

Some bacteria obtain energy from chemical substances in their environment.

🐾 Heterotrophs

Animals and fungi are heterotrophs. Animals obtain nutrients by eating other organisms. Fungi break down organic matter and absorb nutrients.
